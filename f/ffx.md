==🔴Berserk Ward== 50% to prevent the Berserk status effect. Customized using 8 hypello potions stolen from Shred in the Calm lands or dropped by Raldo and Swamp Mafdet.
- Useful against bosses like the Spectral Keeper, whose Bersek tail has 50% infliction rate. Combining rates results in zero.

==🔴Deathtouch== 50% base chance to instantly kill a vulnerable enemy. It has no effect on zombie status. Lulu is the primary candidate for this ability, as her weapon fatal cait sith is purchasable in Moonflow. It most valuable for monster arena capturing or grindin in areas like inside sin and the omega ruins, where it pairs well with capture or mug abilities.

==🔴Dark ward== 50% to resist status darkness (blind)

==🔴Firestrike== adiciona o elemento fogo aos ataques físicos e habilidades do personagem. Pode ser adicionada usando 4 bomb fragments. Se o inimigo for fraco ao fogo, o dano aumenta em 50%.
- Multiplos elementos: O jogo seleciona automaticamente o elemento mais favorável ao inimigo.

==🔴Ice ward== reduces ice elemental damage by 50%. Custom: 4 antartic winds stolen from enemies like white elements and snow flan. Superior to NulFrost and inferior to iceproof and iceeater

==🔴Initiative== raises chances of preemptive strike (acting before enemies) and eliminates ambushes in random encounters. Custom: 6 chocobbo feathers STOLEN from Qactuar in the thunder plains or cactuar enemies in bikanel island.

==🔴Lightning ward== reduces lightning elemental damage by 50%. using 4 electro marbles stolen from enemies like thunder flans or found in chests.

==🔴Poison ward== 50% chance to resist the poison status effect. Stronger alternatives: 
- Poisonproof customized with 12 poison fangs, this ability grants immunity to poison.
- Ribbon An acessory that provides status immunity to all ailments.

==🔴Sensor== reveals current/total hp, weaknesses, resists and status effects of target

==🔴Sleepstrike== habilidade automatica de arma que adiciona a condição sleep. Custom: pode ser adicionada a qualquer arma usando 16 dream powders. Sinergia: combinar sleepstrike com sleeptouch aumenta a chance para 150% e a duração para 6 turnos. Fonte: sinspawn gui ou sleep sprout na arena de monstros.

==🔴SOS NulShock== elemental immunity ONLY during critical health. Seek better alternative like `thunder ward`.

==🔴Water ward== reduces water elemental damage by 50%. Unlike Waterproof that grants 100% immunity. Source: 4 fish scales from water flan, aqua flan, octopus, sahagin, crawler and Spherimorph. Useful for mitigatin damage from enemies like the extractor, sinspawn echuilles and Lord OCHU.

==🔴Thunder ward== reduces water elemental damage by 50%. Essential on thunder plains. Requires 4 marbles. Electro Marbles can be stolen from enemies like the yellow element or found in chests.
- for full immunity, players can craft Lightningproof using 8 lightning marbles. A shield with lightningproof is also available for Tidus near the Thunder Plains Agency.

## Weapon Meta

While Celestial Weapons are the best in Final Fantasy X because of their hidden defense-ignoring ability, custom weapons are essential for specific goals like AP farming or utility builds
https://www.reddit.com/r/FinalFantasy/comments/13kgx1m/how_do_you_decide_what_customize_your_weapons_and/


1. The Best AP-Farming Weapon (Endgame)

To quickly max out the Sphere Grid using the Don Tonberry trick, customize a weapon with:
• Triple AP
• Overdrive → AP
• Triple Overdrive


2. General Endgame Custom Weapon Builds

If you are crafting custom weapons instead of using Celestial Weapons, the setup depends on your character's role:

• Mandatory Core Ability:
	• Break Damage Limit (Allows damage to break past 99,999)
• For Physical Attackers (Tidus, Wakka, Auron, Kimahri):
	• Break Damage Limit
	• Evade & Counter (Counters physical attacks even if you fail to dodge)
	• Piercing (Ignores enemy defense, though less necessary if Strength is maxed out)
	• Strength +20% or Triple Overdrive
• For Magic Casters (Yuna, Lulu):
	• Break Damage Limit
	• One MP Cost (Crucial for spamming high-level spells or Quick Hit)
	• Magic Booster (Increases magic damage at the cost of higher MP, paired with One MP Cost)
	• Magic +20%
• Utility / Special Weapons:
	• Capture (Purchased from the Monster Arena to catch fiends for unlocks)
	• First Strike (Essential on at least one party member to handle ambushes like Great Malboros in the Omega Ruins)
	• Gillionaire / Master Thief (Rikku-specific utility for gil or rare item drops)

## Meta Armor


Variações e Alternativas Populares

• Para o Dark Yojimbo / Lutas específicas: Substitua o Auto-Phoenix por Defense +20% em um dos personagens, pois contra o Yojimbo o ideal é sobreviver aos ataques em vez de depender apenas de reviver.
• Para o Penance: Muitos jogadores preferem a build: Auto-Haste, Auto-Protect, Auto-Potion e Defense +25% (ou Auto-Regen), usando X-Potions com a habilidade Distiller/Auto-Potion para curar a party inteira rapidamente sem gastar turnos com magias.
• Com Break HP Limit (BHPL): Se você optar por treinar HP até 99.999 (o que exige dezenas de horas extras de grinding de spheres), a build muda para: Break HP Limit, Auto-Haste, Auto-Regen e Ribbon. No entanto, a build padrão sem BHPL (9.999 HP) é muito mais eficiente e exige menos tempo de jogo.


Onde conseguir as armaduras e materiais

• Armaduras com 4 slots vazios: Podem ser compradas com o Wantz em Macalania Woods (desde que você tenha falado com ele antes no Monte Gagazet) ou dropadas em batalhas nas Omega Ruins.
• Materiais de Customização:
	• Auto-Haste: Chocobo Wing x80 (recompensa por capturar monstros nas Calm Lands ou suborno/bribe).
	• Auto-Phoenix: Mega Phoenix x20 (obtido no Monster Arena ou cofres).
	• Auto-Protect: Light Curtain x70 (obtido contra o chefe Fafnir no Monster Arena).
	• Ribbon: Dark Matter x99 (dropado/recompensa do Monster Arena ou do truque do Dark Yojimbo)6
